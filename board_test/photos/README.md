# Contact-experiment setup photos

**Kind: Photo.** These eight photographs show the experimental setup and the
motion sequence of the contact-maintenance experiment on the 6-DOF arm. They are
the source images of Fig. 6 in the paper: sponge on top, rigid plate below.

The photos are a visual record only. They do not carry or support any measured
value. The force readings that are visible on the small display of the
thin-film force-sensor readout are not data of record and should not be read off
the images.

| File | Surface | Panel in Fig. 6 | Phase |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sponge_a_approach.jpg` | sponge | top (a) | approach |
| `sponge_b_descent.jpg` | sponge | top (b) | descent |
| `sponge_c_contact.jpg` | sponge | top (c) | contact with the thin-film force sensor |
| `sponge_d_holding.jpg` | sponge | top (d) | holding phase |
| `rigid_plate_a_approach.jpg` | rigid plate | bottom (a) | approach |
| `rigid_plate_b_descent.jpg` | rigid plate | bottom (b) | descent |
| `rigid_plate_c_contact.jpg` | rigid plate | bottom (c) | contact with the thin-film force sensor |
| `rigid_plate_d_holding.jpg` | rigid plate | bottom (d) | holding phase |

Visible in the frames: the arm (joints J1–J6), the Zynq-7100 FPGA board behind
it, and the thin-film force sensor with its readout. In the paper figure, panel
(a) adds joint markers and boxes on top of these photos. The files here are the
unannotated originals.

Processing: each image was converted from its embedded Display P3 colour profile
to sRGB and saved again from its pixel data alone (JPEG, quality 92). This removes
all EXIF and camera metadata and the embedded profile. Nothing was cropped,
retouched or resized; every image is 1702 × 1276 px, as in the source.
